What the figures on this site mean

Numbers in the case studies come from audits of real plants and are presented anonymised. They are identified opportunities, not realised savings, and they are specific to the plant, the process and the tariff they came from. Each case study says so on its own page.

Industry figures quoted elsewhere — penalties, surcharge rates, typical ranges — are taken from public records and published sources as at the date shown, and are illustrative. None of it is a forecast of what your site would achieve, and none of it is a commitment.
