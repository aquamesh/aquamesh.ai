Cookies

No tracking cookies. One stored preference.

The whole cookie position on this site, which is shorter than most: there is nothing measuring you.

Last updated 23 September 2026 · AquaMesh Inc.

What this site sets

Two things, and only one of them can be switched off:

WhatSet byPurposeLifetimeOptional?
am-consent, am-optout-saleAquaMesh (local storage)Remembering your cookie choice and your opt-out, so we do not ask againUntil you clear site dataStrictly necessary
_ga, _ga_<ID>Google Analytics 4Distinguishing one visit from another and measuring which pages are readUp to 2 years; data retained 14 monthsYes — only if you accept

There are no advertising cookies, no remarketing tags, no pixels, no session recording and no A/B testing on this site.

Nothing runs before you choose

Google Consent Mode v2 is set to denied for analytics storage, advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation before any tag loads. If you reject, it stays denied and no analytics cookie is written. If you accept, analytics storage is granted and nothing else changes — advertising stays denied either way, because there are no ad tags here.

We also run Analytics with Google Signals off and ad personalisation disabled, so the data cannot be used to build an advertising profile or follow you between devices.

Fonts

Typefaces load from Google Fonts. That sets no cookies, but the request itself discloses your IP address to Google on every page. It is the only other third-party request the site makes, and we mention it because it would be easy not to.

Changing your mind

Use the opt-out page to turn analytics off, or clear this site’s data in your browser and the notice will appear again on your next visit. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we apply the opt-out automatically and you will not see a banner at all.
