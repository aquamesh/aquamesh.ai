Cookies

No tracking cookies. One stored preference.

The whole cookie position on this site, which is shorter than most: there is nothing measuring you.

Last updated 23 September 2026 · AquaMesh Inc.

What this site sets today

No tracking cookies. No advertising cookies. No analytics cookies. There is no analytics package on this site, so there is nothing to consent to for measurement purposes.

The only thing stored on your device is a single local-storage item recording whether you dismissed or declined the cookie notice, so that it does not reappear on every page. It holds one word. It contains no identifier, it is not readable by anyone else, and it never leaves your browser.

Why there is a notice at all

Because the site makes one third-party request — fonts from Google — which discloses your IP address to Google, and because we would rather tell you that plainly than not mention it. Declining stops nothing today, since nothing non-essential is running; it records your preference so that if we ever do add measurement, it stays off for you.

If that changes

If we add analytics in future it will be gated on the choice recorded here, it will be named on this page before it goes live, and the notice will describe what it actually does. We will not quietly swap a notice for a consent banner and call it the same thing.

Clearing it

Clearing site data in your browser removes the stored preference, and the notice will appear again on your next visit.
