AquaSpectra™ smart probe

Know when your product or process changes. Before it costs you.

A rugged in-situ probe that reads the full spectral fingerprint of your process water, continuously — so a change shows up as it happens, not in tomorrow's lab result.

  • Protect product Catch carryover, composition drift or residual material before it becomes rework.
  • Control the process Track batches, transitions and CIP endpoints — and run closer to target.
  • Reduce waste and risk See product loss and organic load heading for the drain.
  • Calibrated values when you need them BOD and COD in-situ, calibrated to your own reference samples.

UV/Vis, with optional NIR and Raman. Works across starches, sugars, dairy proteins, fats and oils, beverages, sauces and cleaning chemistry — or calibrated to your own product.

Industrial water is too important for a black box. AquaMesh keeps your team in control of every change.

  • Advisory by default

    Nothing changes on your plant without an operator's approval. Closed-loop control is opt-in, and reversible.

  • Every change on the record

  • Alarms that reach a person

    Critical excursions escalate by phone down your on-call list until someone acknowledges.
