Privacy

What this site does — and does not — collect.

A marketing site with no accounts, no forms and no analytics. Here is exactly what happens when you visit, in plain terms.

Last updated 23 September 2026 · AquaMesh Inc.

The short version

This is a marketing website. It has no accounts, no login, no forms and no analytics. We do not track you across the web, we do not sell data, and we do not use advertising cookies. If you email us, we hold that email so we can reply to it.

What happens when you visit

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records technical request data including your IP address, the pages requested and your browser user-agent, and uses it to serve the site and to protect it from abuse. That processing is GitHub’s, under its own terms.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. That request goes directly from your browser to Google and discloses your IP address to Google. It sets no cookies, but it is a transfer of data to a third party, and it is the only third-party request the site makes.

Nothing else runs. No analytics, no tag manager, no pixels, no embedded video or social widgets, no chat tool, no A/B testing, no session recording.

What we store on your device

One item, and only if you interact with the cookie notice: a record of whether you accepted or declined, kept in your browser’s local storage so we do not ask again. It contains no identifier and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

See the cookie policy for the detail.

If you contact us

Every contact route on this site is a plain mailto: link, so writing to us is an ordinary email. We receive your address, your name if you give it, and whatever you choose to put in the message.

We use it to reply, and to continue the conversation if you want one. We keep business correspondence while the relationship is live and for a reasonable period afterwards. We do not add you to a marketing list from a single enquiry, and we do not pass your details to anyone else for their own marketing.

Plant data, if we work together

This policy covers the website. If you send us operational data — a historian export, tag lists, lab results — that is handled under the agreement we sign with you, not under this page. In general: your data stays yours, it is isolated per customer at the database level, it is not used to train anything shared with another customer, and it comes back to you in open formats whenever you ask.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, or to receive a copy. Email info@aquamesh.ai and we will answer. We will not charge you for it and we will not make you create an account to ask.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — there is nothing on this site that could.

Children

This site is aimed at industrial and municipal operators. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes, and who to ask

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Questions go to info@aquamesh.ai, which reaches a person, not a ticketing system.
