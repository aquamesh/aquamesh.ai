How we work in a plant

The guarantees that matter when your vendor is small.

Being small should cost you nothing. These are the terms AquaMesh works under, and they are designed so that the worst case for you is that you keep your data and go back to how you ran yesterday.

  • Advisory by default

    AquaMesh recommends; your operators decide. Nothing writes to your control system unless you turn that on, per process, after validation — and you can return to advisory at any time.

  • Read-only to start

    The first engagement runs on an export or a read-only connection to the data you already keep. No new hardware, no changes to your network, nothing to rip out if you stop.

  • Your data, isolated

    Each customer's data is walled off at the database level, so one plant's readings are never visible to another. Your data stays yours, and it comes back to you in open formats whenever you ask.

  • Escalation that reaches a person

    Critical findings escalate by phone, down your on-call list, until somebody acknowledges. Not an email into a queue.

  • Everything on the record

    Every recommendation, approval and setting change is logged — who, what, when, and the value before — so an auditor or a new operator can reconstruct what happened.

  • If we ever go away

    Nothing AquaMesh runs sits inside your control loop, so there is no unwinding to do. You keep your history, your findings and your reports, and the plant runs exactly as it did before we connected.

What we don't do

  • We don't take control of your plant.
  • We don't require you to replace instruments or controls.
  • We don't sell, pool or train shared models on your data.
  • We don't quote a saving we can't show the evidence for.

What to ask us for

Before a pilot, most plants want the same things. Ask, and they come back the same week:

  • A walk through a finding on your own data, start to finish.
  • The data-handling summary: what we read, where it sits, who can see it.
  • A reference call with a plant that has run the audit.
