What this does

Switching this on records an opt-out in this browser and turns off Google Analytics for you. Google Consent Mode is set to deny analytics storage, advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation, so no analytics cookies are written and no identifiers are set.

It applies to this browser on this device. If you use another browser, another device, or clear your site data, you will need to set it again — that is a limitation of a browser-based opt-out, not a choice we made.

Do we actually sell or share your information?

No. We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Google Analytics runs with Google Signals off and ad personalisation disabled, specifically so that it does not feed an advertising profile.

We provide this control anyway, because analytics on a website is the kind of processing California law gives you the right to refuse, and because you should not have to take our word for how a third-party tag is configured.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser or an extension sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a valid opt-out request and apply it automatically on arrival. You do not need to visit this page or click anything.
