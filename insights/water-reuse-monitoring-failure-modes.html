Industrial water

Field note · Industrial water reuse · September 29, 2026

Match the signal to the failure you need to catch.

A reuse line can have plenty of measurements and still leave an operator without an answer. The starting question is specific: which developing condition would change a routing, treatment or maintenance decision while there is still time to act?

This is an operating framework, not a report of AquaMesh performance at a customer site. Requirements vary with water source, end use and permit.

Start with the use

One reuse system, several different questions.

A flow balance can show that recovered water is being produced without reaching a use point. It cannot establish that the water is suitable for that use. A quality reading at the treatment outlet can show a process change, but it does not identify which valve or demand change caused a routing loss. The operator needs both views on the same timeline.

The end use sets the required treatment and monitoring context. As one example, EPA’s September 2026 summary of California rules for recycled municipal wastewater distinguishes industrial cooling that creates mist from cooling that does not; its table also identifies continuous turbidity monitoring for certain disinfected tertiary uses and separate coliform sampling. That state-specific summary is not a universal specification for every industrial process water loop. EPA: California industrial reuse summary.

Choose the evidence

What does each measurement actually establish?

  1. 1

    Flow, level and valve state

    Use these with production and demand records to locate a water balance or routing problem. Check meter health and whether measurements represent the same operating interval before calling the difference a loss.

  2. 2

    Pressure and turbidity

    Use treatment equipment status and appropriate quality tags to flag a filtration change. An alarm should point to the affected train, its operating mode and the approved response. A turbidity signal is not a direct microbial measurement.

  3. 3

    Laboratory and microbiological evidence

    Keep the prescribed sampling and methods authoritative. A 2026 Water Research Foundation project tested automated ATP monitoring at a full-scale non-potable reuse facility during normal operation and simulated treatment failures. Its public abstract reports detection of simulated membrane failures within minutes and describes ATP as a complement to conventional laboratory monitoring. That result is evidence for the studied application, not validation of an optical probe or a substitute for a site’s compliance method. WRF project 5319.

  4. 4

    Composition at a decision point

    If the available tags show that a stream changed but cannot distinguish the physical condition that determines whether it can be reused, define that gap precisely. A targeted spectral measurement might be tested for a suitable chemical or organic signature, with paired laboratory samples, a defined range, fouling checks and false-alarm review. It must not be presented as a pathogen test or as a validated concentration before that validation exists.

A testable operating study

Work backward from the decision.

For a hypothetical reuse train, pick one event that would trigger a hold, diversion, inspection or return to service under the plant’s own procedures. Reconstruct the event using timestamped SCADA and historian tags, equipment and maintenance records, production demand, and the laboratory sample collection and result times. Include normal runs for comparison.

At each point, write down what the operator knew then, not what became clear days later. Test whether the existing instruments give enough warning and a likely cause. If they do, improve the decision route and verification. If they do not, specify the missing physical observation and test a sensor at that location. Route any proposed response for operator approval, then check the downstream trend and reference sample against the expected result.

A useful alert names the condition, evidence, owner and action window. The measurement earns its place when it improves that decision and the result can be checked.
